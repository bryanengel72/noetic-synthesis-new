The Seats

Four advisors and one adversary.

Each seat holds one job and is prohibited from doing the others'. That's what keeps five voices from collapsing into one.

  1. 01 Systems

    Connections

    What moves when this moves.

    Maps the interconnections, feedback loops, and second-order couplings your decision touches — the structure of the situation, before anyone judges it.

  2. 02 Risk & Ethics

    Consequences

    Who bears the cost.

    Surfaces the pitfalls, unintended consequences, and ethical weight — who wasn't in the room, and what precedent the decision quietly sets.

  3. 03 Implementation

    Execution

    How this actually gets built.

    Takes the decision as given and works out what it costs to do: sequence, dependencies, technical feasibility, and the first real obstacle.

  4. 04 Innovation

    The Other Route

    The version nobody proposed.

    Explores the creative alternatives and unconventional pivots — the adjacent moves you haven't considered because you were busy defending the original.

The Adversary

How this dies.

Attacks the idea outright — hidden flaws, catastrophic failure scenarios, the quiet ways it fails slowly. Not negativity: the stress test that finds blind spots before you commit resources.

Why five different intelligences: each seat runs on a different AI model family with different training and different priors. Five copies of one model share one disposition and drift toward polite agreement. Five genuinely different minds do not — and the disagreement is the product.

The Deliberation

You see the debate, not just conclusions.

Round One

Positions

Each advisor evaluates your decision independently, from its own seat, blind to the others. Five first readings, uncontaminated.

Round Two

Cross-Examination

Each advisor reads the other four and responds — conceding, holding, sharpening. Where positions move is signal; where they refuse to move is louder.
